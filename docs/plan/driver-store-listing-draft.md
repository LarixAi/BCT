## Full description (Play / App Store)

Veyvio Driver is the mobile duty app for authorised drivers on the Veyvio platform.

**What you can do**
- See published duties and what to do next
- Complete daily walkaround and safety checks with photo evidence
- Report defects and incidents with clear status
- Stay in sync when connectivity drops — pending and failed updates stay visible
- Open navigation to the next stop when your operator uses duty navigation

**What this is not**
- Not a consumer rideshare booking app
- Not a public marketing tool — access is by operator invitation only

**Privacy and support**
- Product privacy: https://veyvio.co.uk/legal/product-privacy
- Support: https://veyvio.co.uk/support · support@veyvio.co.uk
- Website terms: https://veyvio.co.uk/legal/terms

Your organisation remains responsible for operational policies, licensing and passenger safeguarding. Veyvio provides the connected tools Command, Driver and Yard use together.
